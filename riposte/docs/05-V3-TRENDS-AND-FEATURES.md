# Riposte v0.3 · Trend research (2025–26) and what it added

v0.2 gave Riposte a meta layer (collection, packs, talents, quests). v0.3 asks what is working **right now** in roguelike deckbuilders, Balatro-likes and mobile card games, and adds the parts that fit. The rule is the same as before: borrow the reason something is fun, then twist it so it pushes the player toward Riposte's own verb, the chain.

Research date: October 2026. Sources are listed at the end.

## 1. What the market is doing

## 2. What v0.3 adds, and the twist on each

### The Cardsharp, a 4th character (unlocks at level 3)

- **Gilt faction** (22 cards + 2 Legendaries) built on one new resource, the **Fate Coin**. Your coin shows ☀ Sun or ☾ Moon; every Gilt card reads it, and most of them **Flip** it after resolving. It never flips at random, so a Gilt turn is a sequencing puzzle: which face do you want under which card?
- **Ante** is the second half: flips and some cards raise it, and payoffs (Cash Out, Jackpot, Royal Flush) spend it all at once.
- **Twist on borrowed idea:** StS II's "one novel resource per character", crossed with the 2026 dice/luck trend, but with zero randomness. Gilt Reactions read the coin too, so the chain becomes a coin-reading puzzle mid-enemy-turn.

### Alternate acts

- Each act now rolls one of two versions per run: **The Drowned Market** (1B), **The Gilded Masquerade** (2B) and **The Clockwork Abyss** (3B). That is 18 new enemies, with 3 new elites and 3 new bosses (Tide Broker, Impresario, Clockmaker), each with a phase change.
- **Twist:** each B-act leans on a different part of the chain. The Market loves traps, the Masquerade mirrors and Negates, and the Clockwork Abyss punishes slow turns.

### Vows and Heat (opt-in difficulty)

- There are **9 Vows**, unlocked after your first boss kill. Each has ranks:
  - Iron: more enemy HP
  - Fury: Strength
  - Snares: enemies start with traps
  - Poverty
  - Thirst
  - the Hunt: every elite is a Champion
  - the Crown: boss affix
  - Regret: start with curses
  - the Clock: enemies power up on turn 8
- Ranks add up to **Heat**. Heat multiplies score (+10% per point), Coins (+5%) and Season Renown, and pays milestone bonuses at 3/6/10/15.
- **Twist:** Snares is a Vow no other game can have. It hands every enemy a face-down trap, so higher Heat literally means longer chains.

### Omens (tarot consumables)

- There are 14 Major Arcana. You hold up to 3 and use them as a free action. Examples:
  - **The Hanged Man** sets a free Countercurrent.
  - **The Tower** destroys every enemy trap.
  - **Death** applies huge Doom.
  - **The Star** also works on the map.
- They drop from fights (always from bosses) and are sold in the shop.

### Style score

- Every chain scores 10 × length². Negates and kills add flat points. A live **✦ style** meter sits in combat.
- Style feeds the run score and Season Renown.
- **Twist:** Balatro's climbing number, but the thing that climbs is "how well you used the chain".

### Arena (async PvP) and deck codes

- You draft a 16-card deck: a 6-card base plus 10 picks of 1-of-3, with rares at picks 5 and 10.
- You duel saved decks at 60 HP until **7 wins or 3 losses**. Rivals are your own Legacy decks, imported friend codes, or generated duelists who get tougher by tier (Bronze → Grandmaster).
- **Rival's Pick:** after a win, choose one of 3 face-down cards from the deck you just beat.
- **Deck codes** (`RIP1.…`) let players trade decks by text. An imported deck becomes an Arena rival and can show up as an Echo elite.
- No server is needed. Online play would only change where rival decks come from.

### Weekly Gauntlet

- One fixed seed per ISO week for everyone, with a fixed character, 3 Vows and a **Twist**:
  - Hall of Mirrors (every elite is an Echo)
  - Gold Rush
  - Chainmail (heal on 3+ chains)
  - Sudden Death
  - Night of Omens
  - Borrowed Legend
- The run is scored and pays a pack: a Legend pack if you win.

### Card mastery

- Every play of a card counts toward its mastery: 5 / 15 / 35 / 70 / 120 plays unlock Bronze, Silver, Gold, Prismatic and Living frames, plus Dust.
- It is purely cosmetic progress, earned by playing. This is Marvel Snap's card levels without the paywall.

### Season 1: The Gilded Masquerade (free track)

- 30 tiers of Coins, Dust, packs and cosmetics. That covers 8 Familiars, 5 card backs and 6 titles.
- Renown comes from every run, scaled by Style and Heat. There is no paid tier.

### Better art and sound (procedural)

- **WebAudio synth** for every sound effect, so the game ships no audio files.
- **The chain plays a melody:** each link is the next note of a pentatonic scale. A 6-link chain is a flourish, and a Negated link is a sour note.
- **Generative music** with a mood per act and a tense boss mode. SFX and music have separate volume sliders.
- Gilt sunburst and coin art, mastery frames, card backs, a Fate Coin badge, the style meter, a familiar perch, a bestiary of every enemy met, and a recent-runs history.

### Balance (see BALANCE.md)

- The Hollow went from 8% to 29% bot win rate at A0 because of three changes:
  - Ossuary Ward was added to its starting deck.
  - Hollow Crown went from 4 to 6 Doom.
  - 5 new Void cards were added: Ossuary Ward, Hollow Echo, Grim Tithe, Death Knell and Soul Lantern.
- The Cardsharp sits at 23%. The Duelist got +4 HP and a 2nd Parry. The Chorus of Knives lost 10 HP. The Clockmaker's gear cap went from 6 to 5.

## 3. What we deliberately did not copy

- **Paid battle pass, energy timers, limited-time paid offers.** These are the three things 2026 player reviews punish hardest, and they would fight the premium pitch.
- **Real randomness in Gilt.** Coin-flip cards that roll dice feel bad in a game about answering. The coin is open information and flips only when a card says so.
- **Live PvP.** Matchmaking needs a server and a player base. Async decks give the same "beat a real person's build" feeling offline.
